📌 Don't lose track of me!

To easily activate automatic translation and configure your preferred languages, make sure to keep the extension icon handy:

  • Click on the puzzle piece icon 🧩 (Extensions) at the top right of your browser.
  • Look for Stream Chat Translator for Twitch - Pardi and click the pin icon to keep it on your toolbar.
